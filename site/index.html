Anatomy of a seedling

Every glyph lives in the same 5×13 plot of soil.

Ågly
  • Canopy · 3px. Where accents grow, so À and à never collide with the line above.
  • Sprouts · 2px. Ascenders and capitals reach 7px above the soil.
  • Stem · 5px. The x-height, where most of a line of text lives.
  • Soil line. The baseline. Capitals sit centred between canopy and roots.
  • Roots · 3px. Descenders of g, j, p, q, y.
1 em = 10pxUse 10, 20, 30px and every pixel lands whole.
